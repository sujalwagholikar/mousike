## Availability and quality

Search, playback, metadata and optional AI depend on external services and networks. Some recordings may be unavailable, changed, removed, region-limited or unsupported. Three audio-quality options choose among available source formats; they do not guarantee lossless audio or improve an intrinsically low-quality source.

Long-video track lists depend on reliable chapters, timestamps or supplied metadata. Review timings rather than treating them as an authoritative discography. AI-generated ordering can be imperfect and is optional. Users are responsible for their own API-provider account, limits and any charges.

## Builds, data and updates

Obtain binaries from releases intentionally published by the maintainer and verify checksums. Filename/version labels alone do not establish authenticity. Development signing is distinct from a trusted production publisher identity. Back up library data before reinstalling, clearing storage or migrating signing identities.

No permanent service availability, zero latency, universal device compatibility, support response deadline or automatic-update commitment is promised. See each release's validation report; previous browser checks are not a substitute for native Windows testing.
