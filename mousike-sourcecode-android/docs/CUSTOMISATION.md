# Customising Mousiké

Open **Settings → Appearance** for the live look preview, presets, themes, accent, surface treatment, cover corners, text size, card density, fonts and Home shelves. Settings apply immediately. Choosing a preset changes presentation only; it retains your taste and playback settings.

## Player and components

Open **Settings → Player & components**.

- **Player layout:** Classic balances artwork and controls, Immersive makes the cover and title larger, and Compact reduces artwork height and spacing.
- **Artwork size:** Small, Default or Large. Artwork always fits in full. The player's scrolling layout accommodates larger text and short screens.
- **Player background:** Artwork uses the existing cover palette, Solid follows your theme, and Accent follows your chosen accent. Artwork ambience can also be disabled in Appearance.
- **Play button:** Accent or White. Context and shortcut rows can be hidden independently. Lyrics, queue and download actions remain available in the overflow menu.
- **Album opener:** Editorial combines a centred cover with a leading title; Gallery centres the larger artwork and metadata; Compact puts a small cover beside the metadata. None starts playback on opening.
- **Song rows:** Choose spacing, artwork and duration visibility. Like and overflow actions stay available.
- **Mini player:** Floating or Edge, with an optional progress line. The selected cover shape also follows through thumbnails, mixes and detail covers; artist portraits retain their circular identity.
- **Navigation labels:** Hide or show names. Icons keep accessible labels in both modes.

## Taste, quality and motion

**Settings → Personalisation** retains profile, languages, favourite artists, genres and discovery balance. Likes, skips and listening activity continue to tune the existing ranker. Optional Gemini remains under AI recommendations and is disabled by default unless you enabled it.

**Settings → Playback & audio** offers Super high, High and Low. A choice applies to the next item, leaving the current audio alone. High targets up to 160 kbps and Low up to 64 kbps; Super high requests the best available original audio. The source may offer fewer qualities and recovery can use a compatible alternate, so these are preferences rather than bitrate guarantees. Existing offline downloads are not re-encoded.

**Prefer YouTube Music songs** prioritises official audio/album recordings, then official music videos, in discovery and search candidates. It does not remove direct links or change an explicitly selected song. Album ordering is preserved. Already cached entries without music-type metadata keep working and receive that metadata on normal catalog refresh.

**Settings → Display & motion** retains supported refresh-rate choices up to 120 Hz, reduced motion and next-song preparation. Actual refresh rate depends on your display and Android's power/display policy.

## Reset and upgrades

**Restore coral & square defaults** restores presentation options while retaining taste, quality, library and downloads. **Reset Mousiké** under Library & privacy is the existing full local-data reset and has a confirmation dialog.

The 3.6.0 upgrade applies Coral and Square once. Further choices persist normally. Install the signed APK over the existing app without uninstalling to retain its local data.
