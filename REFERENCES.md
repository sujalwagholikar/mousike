## Maintenance review

Revisit the pack when dependencies, distributions, signing identities, model/API endpoints, data storage, telemetry, hosting or project ownership change. Replace dated claims with evidence for the exact release; do not carry forward an old test report as if it validated a new binary. Retain original upstream notices and record ownership/provenance for supplied images and media.

Jurisdiction-specific consumer, privacy, tax, business and intellectual-property obligations depend on how the project is operated and distributed. This pack does not claim that every mandatory document for every country/store/business model has been satisfied. Obtain appropriate review before collecting additional personal data, accepting payments or signing distribution agreements.
