MOUSIKE — ARTIST PHOTOGRAPHS

Edit assets/js/artists.js. The mapping is shared by Home, onboarding, Settings
and artist pages. Put replacement JPG/PNG/WebP files in assets/artists and point
the artist name to /assets/artists/your-file.jpg. Prefer square crops with the
performer's face visible. Keep attribution/license information in sources.json.

All 19 original supplied JPGs and both original full SVG logo files are retained.
Where a local photograph is absent, public Wikipedia metadata can provide a
portrait through the same-origin image proxy. Unavailable images use a neutral
fallback; no synthetic photograph or invented artist image is presented as real.
