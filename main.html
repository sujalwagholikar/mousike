Good to know

A few quick
answers.

Is Mousiké free?

The app is free to download and doesn't require an account or subscription. Optional Gemini-enhanced recommendations use your own API key; Google's separate limits or pricing may apply.

Can I listen offline?

Yes. Download tracks first, then play your completed downloads offline. Search, new recommendations and streaming require an internet connection.

Which Android devices are supported?

The included Android APK is intended for ARM64 devices running Android 8.0 or later. The separate Windows installer targets Windows 10 and 11 x64. It's a direct-install development release, not a Google Play or iOS release.

Do I need an AI API key?

No. The app provides personalised recommendations without an AI key. An optional Gemini-powered discovery enhancement can be enabled in the app's settings.

Will updating keep my library?

Usually, installing an APK with the same package name and signing key over your existing version preserves app data. Make a library backup first and don't uninstall your current version; an incompatible signing key will prevent an in-place update.
